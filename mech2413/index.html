"Exam record" counts the sittings (out of 12, May 2013 to Dec 2024) with at least one question part from that chapter, and the total marks of those parts. A part that needs several chapters is counted under the latest of them. Orbits and re-entry parts (no longer examined) are left out.

Torsion (S6) and pressure vessels (S7) had no handout, but both appear in past papers, so their notes are written from the past papers and standard textbooks and are labelled "Beyond the handouts" throughout. Control (C1, C2) is new for 2026/27, so no MECH2413 paper has control questions. Its guides use questions from two related HKU courses instead, each tagged "Related course": MECH2004 Control (6 papers, 2005–2011) and ELEC2245 Control Systems I (4 papers, 2022–2024). Their "exam record" counts parts from those 10 papers. Guide Cx (purple) holds the parts on topics MECH2413 does not teach.

In the last week, read each guide's "Last-minute checklist" and its Ready answer boxes.

2Exam format

  • Format (Dec 2014 to Dec 2024): 3 hours; six questions of 20 marks each, A1–A3 (statics) and B1–B3 (dynamics); answer any five, at least two from each section. That is about 36 minutes per question. May 2013 used an older format ("answer any five", no section rule).
  • Weighting: written exam 60%, assignments 20%, one laboratory 20%.
  • Formula sheet, statics: none has ever been given for Section A. Learn the formulas.
  • Formula sheet, dynamics (Dec 2021 to Dec 2024): relative velocity of a rigid body (\(\vb{v}_B=\vb{v}_A+\vb{\omega}\times\vb{r}_{B/A}\)), \(I=\int r^2\,dm\), and orbit formulas that are no longer needed. It does not give the rocket equation, the normal–tangential (\(a_n=v^2/\rho\)) formulas or any drag law.
  • Changes for 2026/27: orbital dynamics and atmospheric re-entry were removed from the syllabus, so the dynamics section has less to draw on; control was added (two handouts: C1 PID introduction, C2 controller design). How control will appear in the exam, and whether the six-question format stays, had not been announced when this bundle was written. Check Moodle for the current format.
  • Patterns worth knowing: question A1 is usually a mix of short statics parts (supports, determinacy, centroid, principal stresses); when torsion appears it fills most or all of A2 or A3; Dec 2023 and Dec 2024 set the same torsion question with new numbers. Each guide's opening section gives that chapter's pattern.
  • Past papers used: May 2013, May 2014, Dec 2014, Dec 2015, Dec 2016, Dec 2017, Dec 2018, May 2019, Dec 2021, Dec 2022, Dec 2023, Dec 2024 (72 questions, 276 parts). A question set more than once is solved under the newest sitting, with the other sittings listed.
  • Control practice papers (related courses, not MECH2413): MECH2004 Control May 2005, May 2006, Aug 2006, May 2010, Aug 2010, Dec 2011 (2 h, three of four 20-mark questions, no formula sheet); ELEC2245 Control Systems I May 2022, May 2023, May 2024, Dec 2024 (3 h, six questions; formula sheet with second-order step-response formulas and a Laplace table). 178 parts in all. Whether a MECH2413 control question comes with a formula sheet is unknown, so the guides list what to memorise.
