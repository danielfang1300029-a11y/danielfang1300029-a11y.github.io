2If time is short

  1. Ch.3 Projection and the projection drills: 10 sure marks.
  2. Ch.5 Dimensioning, Ch.6 sections and Ch.2 line types, then the find-the-mistakes drawings: 20 marks.
  3. The 9 title-block items (Ch.2, drills).
  4. Ch.4 and Ch.7 calculations, then the quick-pass chapters 1 and 8.
  5. The night before: the exam cram sheet and each chapter's cram sheet.

3Exam format

  • One written test, Mon 30 Nov 2026 (week 14), worth 50% of the course. Assignments 40% and workshop practice 10% make up the rest.
  • Known content, from the lecturer via classmates who attended in Hong Kong: 10 marks projection, 20 marks finding mistakes in drawings, and some marks (number unknown) on the title block ("corner labels"). Nothing else is known.
  • No past papers exist, so every practice question in this pack is written for these notes.
  • No formula sheet is known. Memorise the title-block items, the 1st/3rd angle placement, the line types, the hatching and dimensioning rules, and the gear and fit formulas (each note's cram sheet lists them).
  • Handouts used: Ch.1–4 (Dr C K Chan), Drawing standard 1, Other views, Fit & tolerance (Dr Zulfiqar Ali), Manufacturing (Dr Wei Yan).
