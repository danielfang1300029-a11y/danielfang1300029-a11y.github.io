2Exam format (December)

  • 3 hours; five 25-mark questions; answer four. Q5 (PDEs) has been compulsory in every December paper except 2015.
  • A formula sheet is given: surface and line integrals, flux for \(z=f(x,y)\), Gauss, Stokes, Fourier series coefficients, and reduction formulas for \(\int\sin^nx\,dx\) and similar. It does not give the chain rule, the second-derivative test, Jacobians, div/curl formulas or separation-of-variables results.
  • May sittings use a different format (all questions compulsory, no formula sheet since 2021); their questions are included and labelled.
