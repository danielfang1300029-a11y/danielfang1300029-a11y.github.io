2Exam format

  • 3 hours, 100 marks: Part I linear algebra (50) and Part II complex numbers, probability and statistics (50).
  • No calculators in every sitting since Dec 2022 (and in most older ones), so fractions and square roots are done by hand.
  • Given: a standard normal table \(\Phi(z)\) only. No formula sheet in any sitting.
  • Recent changes: multiple choice arrived in Dec 2024 (Part I: 10 × 2.5 marks, options A–E). Dec 2025 had multiple choice in both parts (10 × 2 each, four options) plus short free-response questions. May 2026 Part II Q7 was ten answer-only short questions. Speed and accuracy on routine methods matter more than before.
