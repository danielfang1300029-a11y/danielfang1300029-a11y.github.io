How to use it
  1. Tap a concept. The arrows light up the concepts you need first (blue) and the ones it leads to (orange).
  2. Learned = you have read its note sections. Practised = you have done its exam-guide questions.
  3. Every concept is open from the start. The arrows only suggest an order; jump in anywhere.
  4. Concepts are grouped by chapter. Lines show what to learn first inside a chapter; an orange + needs S1 tag means it also builds on another chapter (tap to see which concept).
  5. The bar under each concept shows how often it comes up: the share of past papers with a question on it. A full bar means every paper.
  6. Swipe the panel down (or tap ⌄) to shrink it to its title bar and keep browsing the tree; swipe up or tap ⌃ to open it again.
  7. Drag the tree in any direction inside its box, and pinch with two fingers to zoom. Fit shows the whole tree. Scroll outside the box to move the page.
  8. Your ticks are saved in this browser only.
