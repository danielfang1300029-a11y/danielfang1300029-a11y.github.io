2Exam guide and format

Every paper since 2020 has the same format: an online quiz with fill-in numeric blanks, A/B choice blanks, multiple choice (including "how many statements are true") and True/False. There is no formula sheet, and an answer that is nearly right scores nothing, so speed and exact arithmetic matter. Midterms cover roughly Chapters 1–6; Chapters 8–12 appear only on finals.
