ODEs and Laplace are predictable: the same forms return every year. Most frequent: linear first order (O1.2) and undetermined coefficients (O4.1–O4.2). Laplace is usually Q9 (short transforms) and Q10 (a second-order initial value problem).

In every guide, questions run newest first; 2013–2018 papers are tagged older format and listed last.

2Which ODE method?

Go down the list and stop at the first match.

First order (only \(y'\) appears):

  1. Can you split it as \(\frac{dy}{dx}=g(x)\,h(y)\)? → separable: O1.1.
  2. Is it \(y'+P(x)\,y=Q(x)\), with \(y\) to the first power only? → linear, integrating factor: O1.2.
  3. Written as \(M\,dx+N\,dy=0\) with \(\frac{\partial M}{\partial y}=\frac{\partial N}{\partial x}\)? → exact, find \(F(x,y)\): O1.3.
  4. Is it \(y'+P(x)\,y=Q(x)\,y^n\)? → Bernoulli, put \(u=y^{1-n}\): O2.2.
  5. Can it be written as \(y'=F\!\left(\frac yx\right)\) (every term has the same total degree)? → homogeneous, put \(y=vx\): O2.1.
  6. Has a \(y^2\) term with one solution given (Riccati), or \(y'=F(ax+by+c)\)? → Riccati or shift substitution: O2.3–4.

Second order and higher, linear:

  1. One solution \(y_1\) is given and you need another? → reduction of order: O2.5.
  2. Constant coefficients and right-hand side \(0\)? → characteristic equation: O3.1 (higher order: O3.2).
  3. Of the form \(x^2y''+a\,x\,y'+b\,y=0\)? → Cauchy–Euler, try \(y=x^m\): O3.3.
  4. Constant coefficients and the right side is a polynomial, \(e^{kx}\), \(\sin\), \(\cos\) or a product of these? → undetermined coefficients: O4.1. If the trial repeats part of \(y_c\) (a clash) → O4.2. Initial conditions → O4.3.
  5. The right side is not on the trial list (\(\sec x\), \(\tan x\), \(\frac1x\), \(\ln x\), …)? → variation of parameters: O4.4. A Cauchy–Euler equation with a right side → O4.5.
  6. The question says "use the D-operator method" → O4.6 (rare: one older part).
  7. The question says "use the Laplace transform", or the right side switches on at a time \(a\) (a step function) → LT.6–7.

3Exam format

  • May 2019 – Dec 2024: 10 questions, mostly 10% each. Q1–Q5 calculus; Q6–Q10 ODEs and Laplace (Laplace usually Q9–Q10).
  • Dec 2025: Part I = 10 MC × 2 points + 3 free-response × 10 (Q11–Q13); Part II = Q14–Q18, 10 each.
  • May 2026: Q1–Q3 answers only (a limit's answer is a number, \(\infty\), \(-\infty\) or "does not exist"); Q4–Q5 need "detailed arguments"; Q6–Q10 long questions.
  • 2013–2018: same split, more theory: "State the … Theorem", "indicate clearly the theorems you have used".

On new papers much of the calculus is marked on the final answer, so speed and accuracy matter. In long questions write what a marker expects, e.g. check the conditions before L'Hôpital and name each theorem.

The formula sheet: given vs. learn it yourself
  • Calculus: trig identities always. Derivatives of \(\sin^{-1}x\), \(\cos^{-1}x\) (usually \(\tan^{-1}x\)) on most papers, but none in May 2022, May 2023, Dec 2023, Dec 2024, May 2026.
  • Never given: limit laws; \(\frac{\sin x}{x}\to1\), \(\frac{1-\cos x}{x^2}\to\frac12\); L'Hôpital; IVT, Rolle and MVT statements; product, quotient, chain rules; derivatives of \(e^x\), \(\ln x\), \(a^x\).
  • ODEs: a variation-of-parameters hint on recent papers. Not given: trial-solution table, the "multiply by \(x\)" clash rule, the three auxiliary-equation cases.
  • Laplace: a short, sometimes partial table on most papers; none in Dec 2025. Never given: \(\mathcal L(y')=sY-y(0)\), \(\mathcal L(y'')=s^2Y-sy(0)-y'(0)\), the cover-up rule.

Each guide page lists what its topic gets from the sheet. The Dec 2019 file has no sheet.
